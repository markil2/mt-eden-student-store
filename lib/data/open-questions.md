# Menu & policy decisions

Phase 1 is built on the answers below. Menu data lives in `lib/data/menu.ts`, store settings in `lib/data/settings.ts`.

## Confirmed
**Menu**
- Sandwich bread: **Plain, Everything or Cheese bagel**, or **Croissant**. No biscuit.
- Protein (the $8 sandwich and the $10 combo): **Turkey, Chicken sausage or Bacon**. Pick 1–3; the first is included, each extra is **+$1**.
- Cheese (Cheddar or Provolone) is **optional** on every sandwich.
- $10 combo drink: any drink priced $3 or less (OJ, hot coffee, tea, water, lemonade, energy drink, Gatorade, hot chocolate).
- **Bagel with Cream Cheese**: $3, choose Plain, Everything or Cheese.
- **Chips** ($2): Cheetos Puffs, Sour Cream & Onion, Ruffles or Doritos.
- **Italian Soda** $5 (whipped cream optional, free). **Smoothie** $6. **Hot Chocolate** $3.
- **Hot Coffee & Cold Brew**: Creamer and Sugar packets, none or 1–3 of each, free (also offered with the combo's coffee).
- **Tea** ($3): required flavor from a dropdown: Chamomile, Chai Spice, Peppermint, Double Bergamot Earl Grey, Moroccan Mint, Lemon Ginger. The combo offers the same dropdown (optional) when tea is the drink.
- **Menu sections**: "Coffee, Tea & Hot Chocolate" (cold brew, hot coffee, tea, hot chocolate, chai latte); "Beverages" keeps the cold drinks.
- **Homepage photo**: the croissant breakfast sandwich (`public/photos/croissant-breakfast-sandwich.jpg`).
- **Energy Drink** marked sold out (staff can restock it in Admin → Menu).
- **Muffin**: $3. There's no "muffin of the day".
- Customers make every required choice themselves. Nothing is preselected.

**Store**
- Hours: about 30 minutes before and after school; exact times vary.
- Last order is 8 AM, and it applies to after-school orders too.
- Pickup spot: resting room, E Hall.
- Customers choose Before school or After school. There's no limit on orders per window.
- Contact: Instagram @mtedenentrepreneurship only (no phone or email on the site).
- The cafe is described as "Run by Entrepreneurship".
- Classroom delivery is open to any room, and students type their own room number. No delivery fee.
